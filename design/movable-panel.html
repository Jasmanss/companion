Closing a panel you moved

What happens

Drag the top barPress on the Pill, the name or the empty space and move more than 5 points. The panel follows and never leaves the work area (the dashed box under "Show the zones"). The buttons and the project chip still click.
Open where you left itOnce you have moved it, every way of opening it (the tab, the notch, an alert, the menu bar icon, ⌥ Space) pops it in at that spot: a fade with a small grow. Until you move it, nothing changes.
Put it backDouble-click the top bar, or Settings → Steward → Reset. It glides home and the old entrance from the edge or the notch comes back.
ClosingA moved panel shrinks and fades where it is, the pop-in in reverse, and then the tab or the notch is there as usual. It does not fly across the screen. With Reduce Motion it fades.

Where it works

CaseWhat happens
Only when needed (quiet mode)Nothing hides while the panel is open. After it closes, the tab comes and goes at the edge as today, and a click on it opens the panel at the saved spot.
Keep panel open (pin)A pinned panel stays where it is. Moving it does not unpin it.
In the notchBefore the first move the panel drops from the notch. After it, it opens in place. Close it in place. The notch is the character at rest as before. Reset brings the drop back.
Alerts and the Visual Review cardThe alert still comes from the edge or the notch. A click on it opens the panel at the saved spot. The card is inside the panel, so it is wherever the panel is.
Stepping aside, full-screen appsAs today. The spot is kept as a fraction of the screen's work area, so it stays on screen if the Dock or the resolution changes.
A second screenThe spot is saved per screen. It opens on the screen where you last left it, if that screen is still connected. If not, it falls back to today's placement and the saved spot waits for the screen to come back.
