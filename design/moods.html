How it works

  • Each eye is a pill cut by two lids. The top lid drops and tilts: inner corners down is mad, inner corners up is worried. The bottom lid pushes up into an arch for happy.
  • Every value springs to its new target with a little overshoot, so a change of mood pops instead of sliding.
  • It blinks on its own (sometimes twice), follows your cursor when idle or listening, scans while thinking, and bounces its eyes while talking.
  • Blocky uses hand-drawn pixel eyes for each mood rather than a shrunk copy, because a pixel eye only has a few cells to work with.
  • Reduce motion keeps each mood's face but drops the blinks, glances, springs and body moves.
