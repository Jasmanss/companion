Pill

One character, three ways to draw it: in Liquid Glass, flat (one solid color), and blocky (pixels). Every option below applies to all three, so you can compare them side by side.

What else the Pill can do

Ideas beyond add-ons, for later. Most are about motion, which we are leaving until the look is settled.

  • Stretch to talkThe capsule gets a little longer and shorter with the voice, like the Dynamic Island growing.
  • Become the panelWhen you click it, the pill itself widens into the panel. Same glass, one shape, no jump.
  • Light from insideThe eyes tint the glass around them: warm when it needs you, soft blue when it is thinking.
  • Squash when tuckedResting, it flattens slightly against the screen edge; it rounds out when it wakes.
