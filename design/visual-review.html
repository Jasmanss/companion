Visual Review

"Claude Code changes your code. Steward shows you what changed." When a session stops after changing files in a project with a running app, Steward slides out with a before and after of the page, and two buttons.

A design page for the brief. The pages inside the cards are drawn in CSS. Drag the slider, try the buttons, and compare the three places the card can show.

How it happens

1 You send a promptBeforeThe prompt hook tells Steward. It takes a picture of your running app.
2 Claude worksNothing to doSteward stays quiet. No extra calls, no model.
3 The session stopsAfterThe stop hook tells Steward. It takes a second picture and compares.
4 Something changedThe cardOnly if the page looks different. Otherwise it stays in the history.

The review card

In the panel, at the top, like "where you left off". The slider reveals the page as it was (left) and as it is now (right). The two buttons are the whole decision.

Not every turn makes a card

Nothing looks different

No card, no alert. It is saved in the history. A refactor that does not change the page stays quiet.

The app is not running

No card. The history says "my-site was not running at localhost:3000", so you know why.

No before picture

Steward started mid-turn, or the app was down at the start. It shows only the after, labeled so.

A login screen

Steward does not sign in, so a page behind a login looks the same twice. Version 1 does not support it, and says so in Settings.

Setting it up

One field per project, in Settings → Projects. Empty means Visual Review is off for that project.
