The notch island (Peeking, one joined shape)

Steward peeks out from behind the notch, and one black island grows out of the notch in the size the message needs: compact while Claude runs, a note for a short message, big for an approval or a review. When the island is big, Steward slides in and becomes part of it. The chat stays in the side panel. Live and animated, with the hand-off in slow motion. The earlier tries (three more mascots, the shelf, eight Dynamic Island style directions) are folded under it. It lives at the end of the Showcase (sections 7 and 8).

Steward's main chat: four directions and two mixes

Overnight Watch (go rest, I've got the build)

The second headline feature, designed: a goodnight card, then Steward watches Claude Code sessions, deploys and CI while the Mac is awake. Urgent things call and then text, things waiting on you only text, nothing risky is ever approved. In the morning the brief becomes a night report with Visual Review cards. Click through the goodnight card, the notch island at night, the call and text ladder (pick what happened and how the phone answers) and the report. It also lists what each phase can and cannot catch, the Twilio cost, and exactly what you set up yourself.
