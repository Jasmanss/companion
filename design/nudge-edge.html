The alert card's edge

The "needs you" card on the screen edge reads heavy: a light-blue line along its top and bottom. Four versions of its edge, on the right and left edges, in dark and light, over a busy desktop.

Why it looks thick today. An urgent card has a 1.5 pt ring at 90% glow drawn inside its edge (.nudge.urgent in styles.css). The side against the screen is square and half off screen, so what is left of the ring is a long straight line along the top and the bottom, and the line is brighter than any other edge in Steward. All four versions below stay inside the card, because the window is exactly the card's size and cannot draw outside it.

Light

Recommendation: A, the hairline

A is the same half-point glass rim every other Steward surface has (the panel, the settings window), with a light tint of the glow so an urgent card still reads as a card that wants you, and none of the heaviness. It changes one line of CSS. B is the softest but looks smudged next to the rest of the panel, and C adds a rail on one side, which is a detail the app does not use anywhere else. If the user wants urgency to stand out more than A does, B is the next step up.

Files Main building would change: src/renderer/src/styles.css only, the .nudge.urgent rule. Nothing in the main process, the settings or the shared files.
