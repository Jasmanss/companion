What the research says works

  • It lives at emoji sizeThe peek is 50×44 on a near-black tab. That is smaller than a Dock icon. Most of the time this is the only Steward people see.
  • Silhouette firstFilled solid black, it should still be recognizable. One signature shape (ears, a tail, an antenna) does more than any detail.
  • Fewest shapesDuolingo builds characters from one or two rounded shapes for head and body. Every extra shape muddies the outline when small.
  • Big eyes, set low and wideThe baby schema: a large round head, eyes below the middle, a small mouth. The eyes carry the personality, so they have to survive at 50×44.
  • Strong contrast on darkThe tab is near black, so the body needs a light or bright value. Dark bodies vanish. This is why coucou's white Mochi reads even at 30 px.
  • Redraw the small sizeDon't just shrink. Drop fine details, thicken lines, enlarge the eyes, and check at real size.
  • Stand out from the crowdThe Mac notch is now full of pixel cats and dogs (Mac Pet, NotchMate, Notchling, Cute Notch) plus coucou's blob. A clear theme helps; the name Steward suggests service.
