Searching the web

Four takes on the "Searching the web" step, next to the one the app had first. D, the deck of pages, is the one in the app now. Each card loops one real search: the query arrives, it searches, the sites it found come in, and it finishes with a ✓. Only transform and opacity move, each has a still version for Reduce Motion, and a card off screen holds still. The steps come from shared/activity.js, which the Glass Lab and the Showcase use too.
