Three layouts

The notch is pure black, so the Pill's body becomes that black shape and its color becomes the light in its eyes. Each layout is shown at real size (1 point to 1 pixel, like on the screen) and at 2×, on a light and a dark wallpaper, over a measured 185 × 32 point notch under a 33 point menu bar. Anything drawn under the notch is hidden, as on the real screen. Hover the big screen to see it react.

Every mood

All twelve moods in the layout picked at the top, in all three versions, at real size and at 2×. Layout C brings its black out for anything but idle, resting and sleepy. Layout B moves the face into a chin for thinking and talking, since the dots and the mouth live in the middle.

Six colors

The app's six colors as the light of the eyes. Colors too dark to read on black (Graphite) are lifted until they reach 4.5:1. Glass glows softly, Flat is solid, Blocky is pixels.

Moving in and opening

The two big transitions, on a whole screen, in the layout picked at the top.

From the screen edge into the notch, and back

Edge

The tab stretches into a drop of black, runs up the edge and along the top of the screen, and the notch bulges as it takes it in. Going back, a drop pinches off the notch and runs the other way. Reduced motion: the tab fades out and the notch fades in.

Opening the panel, and tucking it back

A click drops the black down from the notch and grows it into the panel's size; the glass takes over from the black, and the eyes become the Pill in the panel's header. Clicking away or Esc reverses it. Reduced motion: a cross-fade.
