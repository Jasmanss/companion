Notch styles

Today's Wings put one eye at each end of two black arms beside the notch, with a black alert hanging in the middle. It reads as two separate lights. Below: three ways for the wings to turn into the Chin's pair of eyes (A to C), four new styles (D to G), and today's Chin and Wings for comparison.

State Eyes

The wings turn into eyes

Each starts as today's Wings at rest, grows a little on hover, then becomes the Chin with a pair of eyes and an alert line when something needs you. Going back runs it in reverse.

New styles

Each shows its look at rest, on hover, and with an alert. Only the notch, a black shape and lit eyes in your eye color.

Today, for comparison

What I would pick

A pair: D, then C. D, Inside as the resting look: nothing grows, nothing covers a menu bar item, and the notch itself holds the face. C, Hand-off is the simplest way to make Wings turn into eyes, and it is the only one that keeps working unchanged with Reduce Motion. If you want the wings kept as a style, A, Slide is the clearest story (the eyes visibly travel to the chin) and B, Melt is the most alive, but both are the busiest to build and to keep smooth. Wings in any form still cover menu bar items while they are out, which is why I would not keep them at rest.
