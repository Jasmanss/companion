Liquid Glass

Instead of a flat character sitting on top of glass, these are made of the glass. Same material as the panel, with light-up eyes. None of them has the gamepad outline of Discord's logo or the rounded square of coucou's Mochi.

  • LensingLiquid Glass is defined by how it bends what is behind it at its edges. Look at the colored lines behind each one.
  • Light from aboveBright top edges and a softer bottom rim, the same light the system uses everywhere.
  • Rounded, floating formsCapsules and soft curves that nest inside other rounded shapes.
  • It adaptsIts color comes from what is behind it. The eyes switch between dark and light to stay readable.
